Privacy

Your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information will not be disclosed to any third parties unless required to by law.

Enabling cookies is recommended, but not required. Most browsers have options or extensions that allow you block cookies for this or any other site if you so wish.

Google Analytics are used to monitor your activity on the website.

In the future CoachingNotebook may share keywords from your data with third parties for the purposes of serving relevant advertising. No personally identifiable information or full text data will be shared with any such parties.

In the future CoachingNotebook may aggregate application data between users for the purposes of providing useful public or private value added services. No personally identifiable information will be used for this purpose.

Since CoachingNotebook runs on Google infrastructure and uses Google authentication, you should also be aware of Google account privacy policy.
